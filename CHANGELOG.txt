0.1.0-beta.1 — initial private release candidate

- Twelve spells per spread: two columns by three rows on each physical leaf.
- Row-major ordering, native text wrapping and original-sized icon resources.
- Native page animation with corrected overlay depth and book controls.
- Turning-paper thickness prevents old lower-row labels bleeding through at
  touchdown, preserving the spell layout and native animation clip.
- Geometry-derived spell tooltip anchors and narrower spell selection margins.
- Separate pinned Universe 2.0.1 and Heroes 5.5 RC19k main-engine profiles.
- Portable builds from local game inputs; game executable files are read only.
- Windows install/uninstall, runtime launcher and installation verification.
- No forced test-window size, lab maps, localization overlays or personal paths.

Known boundaries: lobby launch, multiplayer and other EXE builds are untested.
